Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about fresh flower ordering & delivery.

How does the 2-hour express flower delivery work? ▾
Our partner florist hubs are stationed across 1,000+ cities in India. Once you place an express order, blooms are selected from our temperature-controlled cold room, assembled fresh by our florists, and dispatched via our dedicated courier fleet within 120 minutes.
Can I get flowers delivered at exactly 12:00 Midnight? ▾
Yes! Select the Midnight 12 AM delivery slot during checkout. Our riders deliver between 11:45 PM and 12:15 AM to ensure the surprise is timed for birthdays and anniversaries.
How do you ensure flowers remain fresh during transit? ▾
Stems are secured with specialised floral hydration sponge gel and breathable packaging that preserves moisture and fragrance throughout the delivery journey.
Can I include a personalized message card? ▾
Yes! Every bouquet and hamper includes a complimentary wax-sealed handwritten message card. You can write your custom text during checkout.
