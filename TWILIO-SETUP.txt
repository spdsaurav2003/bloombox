BLOOMBOX TWILIO SMS OTP SETUP

Your Twilio credentials are kept safe in your private .env file:
- Account SID: (Loaded from .env)
- API Key SID: (Loaded from .env)
- API Client Secret: (Loaded from .env)

TO ENABLE LIVE SMS DISPATCH:

1. Finish creating the API Key in Twilio:
   - In your Twilio Console tab (Create API Key), check "Got it! I have saved my Client ID and Client secret..." and click the blue "Finish" button so Twilio activates the key.

2. Get a Twilio Phone Number (Free on trial):
   - In the Twilio Console, navigate to: Phone Numbers > Manage > Buy a number (or click "Get a phone number" on the Console Home).
   - Once acquired, open .env and add the number in E.164 format (with country code, e.g.):
     TWILIO_PHONE_NUMBER=+1234567890

3. (Trial accounts only) Verify Destination Numbers:
   - Twilio trial accounts can only send SMS to numbers verified on the account.
   - Go to: Phone Numbers > Manage > Verified Caller IDs, and verify your mobile number.

4. (Optional Alternative) Primary Auth Token:
   - On the Twilio Console Home page, click the eye icon next to "Auth Token" to reveal your 32-character Auth Token.
   - You can paste this in .env under TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN=.

TESTING & DEVELOPMENT MODE:
- When SMS cannot be delivered (e.g. while configuring the phone number or on unverified trial numbers), BloomBox automatically logs the 6-digit OTP in the server terminal and displays a test badge in the UI.
- The standard demo OTP 1234 is also accepted as a development fallback.
